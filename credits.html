About Hari & the sounds

This first version gives Hari-Hari a little space: one sustained Sa, a tanpura, or silence, with a 10 or 15 minute timer. The harmonium keyboard is available separately.

Harmonium and the single tone

Harmonium Samples — All Keys and Drones, recorded by Donya Quick at the Euterpea Studio, Yale University. The original recording is released under CC0 1.0.

Hari uses the edited notes by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky from tonejs-instruments, under CC BY 3.0. Upstream edits include trimming, fades, level matching, noise removal, and some pitch correction.

Eleven notes were excerpted, circularly crossfaded for sustain, and level adjusted for Hari. The single tone sustains one of these harmonium recordings. Playback includes measured pitch compensation and transposition between sampled notes. These are processed loops; the original instrument’s attacks and releases are not reproduced.

Tanpura

tanpura.wav by marvman, released under CC0 1.0. Described by its recordist as an acoustic tanpura tuned to C, with first and fifth.

Hari uses an excerpt of Freesound’s public HQ MP3 preview, mixed to mono and crossfaded for looping. The original WAV has not been obtained. Changing pitch also changes the speed and character of this recording.

Install Hari

You can use Hari in Chrome right away. A home-screen icon is optional.

On Android, open Chrome’s More menu, choose Install and create shortcut, then Install. You can also use the Install Hari button when it appears in the app. See Chrome’s installation help.

Wait for Available offline in Hari before expecting it to work without a connection.

The timer

Keep Hari visible while practising. Screen-awake support depends on your phone and browser. The timer follows elapsed wall-clock time, but a locked or suspended browser cannot guarantee an on-time completion sound. It is not a background alarm.

A saved timer can return after a reload. Sound resumes only after you tap; an old completion does not replay its sound.

Stop sound also turns off the finishing bell for the current session; the timer continues silently. To reset a running session, pause it first.

Your marks and settings

Morning and evening are marks you make yourself, independent of the timer. They and your settings stay in this browser. There is no account or online sync; clearing site data removes them.
